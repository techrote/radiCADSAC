## Scope and decisive missing case

B1-E1's verified multiplication deliberately refuses different internal partitions. Two separately C0 rational polynomial sources may represent perfectly valid functions over the *same physical interval* but carry different internal breakpoints. A shared Bernstein-sign-cone certificate can be derived without discarding any genuine knot or pretending that local A2 germ certificates prove open-cell nonvanishing. This fills one exact algebraic-composition gap, not general geometric/physical completeness.

**Admitted:** two finite externally pinned B1 generation-zero base records, with individually checked exact rational power/Bernstein coefficients, ordered contiguous C0 partitions, identical closed global domain, and each leaf POS, NEG or identity-ZERO under B1's weak Bernstein sign cones. Union <=128 refined cells, polynomial product degree <=24 and inherited 1024-bit rational cap. Full original source snapshots are verified from untrusted records; their *actual* external source identities must be independently held by the consumer. An in-process \`Verified\` is not safe to deserialize as a proof.

**Refuse:** different global domains, non-C0 sources, mixed-sign Bernstein vectors, forged/stale external pins or proposed receipt, unsupported degrees and resources, arbitrary trigonometric/nonpolynomial sources and incomplete physical producer custody. A2's knot germ alone, even with a correct order and sided sign, is **not** an open-cell certificate. Refusal means UNKNOWN, never no root.

## Exact infinite-family theorem and proof

Let F and G be two verified continuous rational-piecewise polynomials on the same closed interval [a,b], each with a finite rational ordered partition and a valid POS/NEG/ZERO Bernstein certificate on every original leaf. Let U be the sorted union of *all* original endpoints. For each open U-cell, its interior lies in exactly one original F leaf and one original G leaf. Existing B1 rational \`split\` computes the exact restricted power-basis polynomial and subdivided Bernstein coefficients with de Casteljau convex combinations. Under strict interior cuts this preserves the signed cone and identity-ZERO classification without new leaf proofs; C0 and exact endpoint values are unchanged.

Then the existing B1 product convolution on each aligned U-cell computes exactly F·G, with sign the product of its two nonzero inward signs, or ZERO if either parent is ZERO. The polynomial product of two C0 functions is C0 on U. Every nonzero aligned cell has strict signed value on its *open interior* (the Bernstein basis is positive there); thus the entire product zero-set consists **exactly** of maximal closed identity-zero cells plus zero-valued U endpoints. B1's existing \`events()\` independently labels the latter CROSS, TOUCH or BOUNDARY, excluding endpoints absorbed by identity intervals. This is an exact closure theorem for every admitted source, not an assertion that any source is automatically in the Bernstein cone. A freshly re-derived, externally checked output snapshot pin is needed to attach this result to any later consuming authority.

**Nontrivial infinite family:** for arbitrary c,d in nonnegative rationals, F_c(t)=1+c·t(1−t), G_d(t)=−[1+d·t(1−t)] on [0,1], with F split at 1/3 and G split at 1/2. Both source Bernstein vectors are single-signed on their declared intervals (middle coefficient before sign is \`1+c((L+R)/2−LR)\` for interval [L,R], which is >=1 when 0<=L<R<=1). Their output has exact partition \`[0,1/3,1/2,1]\`, is strictly negative throughout its closed domain, and has no zeros. The result holds for infinitely many c,d; 20 finite choices merely challenge the implementation.

**Prior art/novelty:** B1-C1/C2 already established Bernstein/de Casteljau split and E1 established the multiplication law; their composition by a common rational refinement is classical algebra, not a new polynomial, topology or root-isolation theorem. Bertot, Guilhot and Mahboubi, *A formal study of Bernstein coefficients and polynomials*, Mathematical Structures in Computer Science (2011), DOI https://doi.org/10.1017/S0960129511000090. [#462](https://github.com/techrote/radiCADSAC/pull/462) addresses actual B-spline knot insertion and signed affine covariance; **this checker does not import, reproduce or authenticate those source controls**. [#453](https://github.com/techrote/radiCADSAC/pull/453) handles a separate restricted trigonometric interval isolator. [A2 #471](https://github.com/techrote/radiCADSAC/pull/471) provides the decisive same-knot-germ/different-global-zero-set warning; our mixed-sign extra-root substitution is refused before composition.
