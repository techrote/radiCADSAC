## Hypotheses and falsification

The machine-readable pre-registered hypotheses are in `experiment-plan-v1.json`. The key tests are intentionally capable of falsifying a preferred architecture: STEP writer isolation is judged from serialized uncertainty rather than object ownership assumptions; Boolean validity is insufficient if the material oracle disagrees; and BRepGraph usefulness does not imply that its identifiers become durable programme identity.

## STEP cross-talk minimization

`harness/diff_worker.cpp --probe step` removes the RCS-017 stress scaffolding down to four writes of the same simple solid. Two fresh writers establish sequential controls. The cross-talk case then creates two distinct writers, sets conflicting tolerances (`0.00001 mm` and `0.001 mm`) before either transfer, and transfers the first writer only after the second writer has been configured. Each output is parsed for `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT` and independently read back as a B-rep.

This is deliberately a public-API reproducer. It does not assume that distinct C++ writer objects imply independent controller/actor state. Upstream source shows `STEPControl_Writer::SetTolerance()` writing through `WS()->NormAdaptor()->ActorWrite()` to `STEPControl_ActorWrite::SetTolerance()`, so serialized evidence remains the authority.

## Concurrency ownership probe

`--probe parallel` separates two questions that RCS-017 had to keep distinct:

1. `BRepAlgoAPI_Cut::SetRunParallel(true)` is exercised on one operation while the process-global `BOPAlgo_Options` flag is held false.
2. Two threads deliberately conflict on `BOPAlgo_Options::SetParallelMode`; a deterministic hand-off checks whether one thread sees the other's process-global value.

Even a clean result here cannot remove the process-isolated worker boundary by itself. RCS-017 observed STEP configuration cross-talk and process-global state under a wider campaign; RCS-024 may only narrow or confirm that evidence.

## RCS-007 decisive replays

Two compact manufacturing controls are replayed on both pins:

- **1 µm positive radial skim with 0.1 µm?** No: the exact values are a `0.000001 mm` positive skim with `0.0001 mm` OCCT fuzzy value, matching the previous valid-but-materially-wrong family. The analytic positive removed volume is retained as the material oracle.
- **100-step repeated finish chain:** `0.00001 mm` increments are applied ascending and descending with `0.0001 mm` fuzzy value. The differential records both final-volume errors and the order delta.

The operation journal's positive-removal intent is never erased merely because a Boolean returns a valid topology.

## RCS-011 decisive replays

The `retrace-jitter` geometry is reproduced exactly: two 30 mm horizontal flat-end-mill traversals, radius `2.5 mm`, separated by `0.001 mm`. The segment-by-segment reference is compared with a one-shot n-ary freehand batch. Both outputs are checked for B-rep validity and material volume.

A `slot-clean` dense sampled fallback uses `0.25 mm` spacing and is run by the Python driver under an **8 second child-process bound** on each pin. Timeout is evidence, not a test-harness crash. The original RCS-011 30 second campaign remains the deeper historical control.

## BRepGraph/history practical probe

The same probe is compiled against both exact pins. A box is imported into `BRepGraph`, then the graph-native history layer is exercised with:

- one-to-many `face -> {face, face}` history (`split_image_count == 2`);
- many-to-one `{face, face} -> face` history (`merge_origin_count == 2`);
- a `VersionStamp` captured before `graph.Clear()` and required to become stale;
- a rebuild in a fresh graph with a valid `ItemUID`, recording whether a numerical UID collision occurs across graph instances.

The last observation is intentionally adversarial to accidental identity promotion. `BRepGraph_ItemUID` is compact graph identity that survives compaction/reordering; it is **not** the canonical operation ID, body ID, source identity, or provenance key. A same-number UID in another graph cannot establish semantic sameness.

## Architectural guardrails

Regardless of comparative outcome:

- process isolation remains the founding scheduler boundary unless a later issue performs broad, multi-surface qualification;
- OCCT/BRepGraph UID/history stays backend-private and may annotate or accelerate reconstruction, never replace the canonical manufacturing journal;
- STEP remains mandatory primary engineering output;
- topology validity never overrides material/semantic oracles;
- tolerance is not widened to make a failing fixture pass;
- disconnected material bodies, source identity and provenance cannot be silently discarded.
