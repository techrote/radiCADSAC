## Exact construction

For a v10-qualified one-positive-harmonic span,

`F(s) = A(s) cos(pi*u(s)) + B(s) sin(pi*u(s))`,

`A` and `B` are exact rational polynomials, `gcd(A,B)=1`, and `u(s)` has nonzero exact-rational affine rate `u'`. v10 selects the tangent chart when `B` is exactly zero-free and the cotangent chart when `A` is exactly zero-free.

Let

`D(s) = A(s) B'(s) - A'(s) B(s)`.

Then

- tangent: `H' = pi*u'*sec^2(pi*u) - D/B^2`;
- cotangent: `K' = -pi*u'*csc^2(pi*u) + D/A^2`.

The exact theorem `pi > 3` and `sec^2(x) >= 1`, `csc^2(x) >= 1` imply a rational sufficient condition. With `den=B` in the tangent chart and `den=A` in the cotangent chart, define

`P(s) = 3*abs(u')*den(s)^2 - sign(u')*D(s)`.

If `P(s) > 0` for every `s` in the closed local span, then `H'` has sign `u'` or `K'` has sign `-u'`. Thus every pole-free chart is strictly monotone even if `D` itself changes sign. Exact endpoint/pole signs decide zero or one open event per chart, and every such event is simple.

`P>0` is established only from exact polynomial authority: positive exact endpoint values plus an MC-032/Sturm proof of no open-span root. No decimal or binary approximation of pi, numerical derivative, epsilon, finite sampling, timeout or subdivision cap is a truth source.
