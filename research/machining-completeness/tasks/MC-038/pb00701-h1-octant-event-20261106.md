## Formal sufficient theorem

Let \(C,S\) be rational affine (or constant) functions on a closed source subcell \([a,b]\). Let \(q(t)=C(t)^2+S(t)^2>0\) everywhere, established by the exact quadratic minimum (including a rational interior vertex). Let

\[
F(t)=C(t)\cos(2\pi\beta(t))+S(t)\sin(2\pi\beta(t)),\qquad
\beta(t)=u+rt
\]

with rational nonzero \(r\), and \(\lvert\beta(b)-\beta(a)\rvert=1/8\). Write \(\alpha(t)\) for a continuous argument of \(C+iS\) and \(\delta(t)=2\pi\beta(t)-\alpha(t)\). Define the constant Wronskian

\[
W=C\,S'-S\,C'.
\]

When \(rW\le0\), we have \(\delta'(t)=2\pi r-W/q(t)\) of the **strict** sign of \(r\) throughout the cell. Therefore \(F=\sqrt q\cos\delta\) has only simple physical zeros and the \(\delta\)-ordering agrees with source-time order (or reverses consistently when \(r<0\)).

**Geometric angular-span lemma:** The image \(v(t)=(C(t),S(t))\) is a straight segment disjoint from the origin. Its continuous argument sweeps strictly less than \(\pi\): the segment is contained in an open half-plane through the origin (equivalently its endpoint directions are not antipodal). Thus

\[
|\delta(b)-\delta(a)| \le |\Delta(2\pi\beta)|+|\Delta\alpha|
<\pi/4+\pi=5\pi/4<2\pi.
\]

Two or more consecutive simple zeros of \(\cos\delta\) are separated by \(\pi\) in \(\delta\); three require at least \(2\pi\). Since opposite nonzero physical endpoint signs imply an odd count, **opposite signs imply exactly one root**, with multiplicity one and a strict rational source-time bracket \((a,b)\).

For **same** nonzero endpoint signs, the implementation additionally certifies

\[
\frac{11}{14}+\frac{|W_u|}{q_{\min}}<3<\pi,
\]

where \(W_u=C(0)\Delta S-S(0)\Delta C\) is the derivative with respect to normalized cell parameter \(u\in[0,1]\). This is an exact *sufficient* bound on total phase drift, using \(\pi<22/7\), \(\pi>3\), \(\lvert\Delta(2\pi\beta)\rvert=\pi/4<11/14\) and \(\lvert\Delta\alpha\rvert\le |W_u|/q_{\min}\). It implies **zero roots** because same signs plus strictly monotone drift \(<\pi\) exclude a crossing pair. If the bound is not established, classify \`REFUSED_NONTRUTH\`, never "no root."

At phase index \(k\bmod8\), physical sign equals the sign of one of
\[
(C,\ C+S,\ S,\ -C+S,\ -C,\ -C-S,\ -S,\ C-S).
\]
The factor \(1/\sqrt2>0\) in odd indices cancels **for sign only**; no false rational amplitude or rational root coordinate is claimed. All original source knots must appear among exact nodes, agree continuously and have nonzero physical values. Joined event brackets are disjoint, source ordered, and maximal signed cells are reconstructed from the exact node signs.
