Task: **MC-038 / PB-007-01**. Evidence class: **DETERMINISTIC_MODEL**. Date: 2026-11-03.

original sources left unresolved in the frozen V54 census. It produces forty-nine
finite physical zero sets and one separately typed closed continuum. The finite
records contain ordered physical events, original-source locators and vanishing

The original producers establish existence, uniqueness and completeness.

composition. It does not solve an arbitrary supplied analytic function, repair
a generic owner route, or define a discontinuity policy.

## Hypotheses and falsification criteria

The preregistered hypothesis is that the actual qualified outputs contain enough
source-bound information to normalize all fifty zero sets without a new root
search, arbitrary algebraic-number arithmetic, or changing an original source.
A count or prior COMPLETE label is insufficient: each finite root must come
from an actual event descriptor, except a proved empty strict span, and each
closed exterior and true knot needs its own proof premise.

Falsification includes a descriptor missing from a claimed complete source;
source, whole-row, parameter or affine-phase mismatch; incomplete piece coverage;
an exterior point without a zero/sign decision; a knot with inconsistent sides;
a bracket crossing original pieces; a rational point substituted for an implicit
root; inconsistent smooth order parity; or a nonzero proof cut promoted to a root.

A failed or exhausted producer is nontruth. It cannot become a root-free record,
zero count, or complete fifty-source report. Controls target the actual
proof/adapter interface and composition premises as well as candidate report
bytes.

## Locator and order semantics

A rational event stores a canonical exact coordinate. A nonrational event uses
UNIQUE_IMPLICIT_ZERO_OF_PINNED_ORIGINAL_SOURCE, with original case/hash,
parameter, source-piece index and interval, strict open rational bracket, and
fresh uniqueness-proof field reference. Its defining predicate is the original
physical source equal to zero. Bracket endpoints are not asserted to be roots
or exact root coordinates.

This finite scope needs no exact irrational algebraic physical coordinate.
V44's sqrt(2)/2 is an algebraic orientation proof cut with proved nonzero physical
value. It remains a cut; the physical zero stays in the bracket (9/16,37/64).

An upper locator bound strictly below the following lower bound proves strict
order. Equality also proves strict order if at least one locator is open at
that bound. The ratio cases' adjacent brackets may therefore share one-half
or seven-eighths, and V27 tail100 brackets may meet at nonzero knot one. Two exact
rational events at the same coordinate are duplicates and refuse. A bracket can
begin or end at an original piece edge because its root is strictly inside;
a bracket crossing a source knot does not identify one analytic piece and refuses.

Orders have three meanings:

- A strict-span interior event carries a smooth positive integer order. Odd
  orders reverse the physical sign; even orders preserve it.
- An exterior zero carries only its inward one-sided order. Its outside sign
  and outside order are null.
- A true-knot zero carries separate left and right one-sided orders, with
  global smooth multiplicity explicitly unclaimed. Equal sided orders do not
  assert differentiability across the knot.

V8 double zeros preserve positive signs on both sides. V6 tail100 has a nonsmooth
touching zero at knot one, sided orders one and one, and positive signs on both
sides. Smooth odd-order parity must not be applied across that knot. V23/V25
preserve unequal first derivatives and separate sided orders. V6 tail0 retains
its qualified knot-order scope even though its particular formula admits further
smooth facts.

Two local endpoint records at a zero knot correspond to one physical coordinate.
The adapter checks the existing deduplication proof and sided records; the
assembler permits exactly one physical event there. It never counts two local
records as two original-source roots.

## Maximal connected nonzero cells

For a continuous source on a closed interval with finitely many zeros, removing
each source-interior zero separates the nonzero set. An exterior zero creates no
empty outside cell. Therefore:

**Connected nonzero cells = 1 + source-interior zeros.**

Here source-interior zeros total 81-2=79, including the four knot zeros.
Across forty-nine finite sources the cell total is 49+79=128. Strict-span
counts exclude knot events and total seventy-five; these counts are distinct.

The assembler creates boundaries from physical exteriors and physical event
references. It never uses an isolating bracket bound as a cell boundary. Outer
nonzero exterior points are included; zero exteriors and interior zeros are
excluded. Every cell carries a strict physical sign, original piece indices,
and the nonzero true knots crossed inside that connected region.

A touching zero disconnects the nonzero set even when the signs agree. V8 smooth
touches and V6 tail100's nonsmooth touch retain separate same-sign cells.
Conversely, a continuous nonzero knot joins equal-sign pieces into one connected
cell. Ratio poles, certificate subdivisions and V44 rational/algebraic proof
cuts do not split physical nonzero cells.

The assembler independently checks piece containment, strict ordering, smooth
parity, sided knot scope, exterior/knot decisions, producer counts, adjacent sign
compatibility and producer sign-cell sequences. This is conditional composition
of attested primitives, not an alternate public route for alleged caller proofs.

## Closed continuum is a separate type

The continuum has CLOSED_CONTINUUM_ZERO_COMPONENT, the whole closed original
interval, every-point-zero semantics and uncountable cardinality. Its isolated
root count is null. Finite event lists, sign-cell lists and isolated vanishing
orders are inapplicable, represented by false applicability flags and null lists.

It is never an empty finite zero set, a pair of isolated endpoint roots, or a
zero-count success. Original span identity and source edges are consumed
explicitly. No finite normalization loop processes it.

## Bounds and refusal behavior

The admission envelope is fifty exact sources, at most two original pieces and
eight isolated roots per source. These bounds cover the actual population and
are explicit finite scope, not general numerical limits.

Public JSON uses exact built-in types, a two-MiB aggregate budget, bounded
depth/nodes/containers/strings/integers, and rejects cycles, shared container
aliases, custom subclasses, floats and nonfinite values. Canonical bytes keep
Booleans and integers distinct. Report pathname text is bounded to 4096 characters
before path construction/read, with empty strings and NUL rejected. File read
failures produce typed refusal.

Each callable rational parser checks ASCII integer/ratio grammar, at most 128
characters and numerator/denominator bit lengths at most 256 before Fraction.
Exponent, decimal, leading-plus and oversized syntax never reaches that
constructor. Short bounded construction precedes a reduced canonical spelling
check; for example 2/4 is safely refused as noncanonical. No caller report string
is passed to an old producer's rational parser.

A producer refusal, exception, pin mismatch or budget failure aborts the complete
report. There is no saved-success fallback. The saved v2 report is a comparison
target and original receipt admission reference after actual execution, not a
substitute for the proof call.
