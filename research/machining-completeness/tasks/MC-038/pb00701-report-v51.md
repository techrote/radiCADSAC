## Question, hypothesis and falsification

Can the already qualified v50 physical product `F=gH` acquire a complete ordered
root/sign-cell decomposition without modifying its source or count certificate?
The bounded carrier has a checked consistent strict derivative and at most one
open simple root. V50's source-derived GCD and scoped irrational disjointness
permit exact carrier-sign refinement at factor-owned algebraic roots.
The sign identity `sign(delta*H(x))=sign(x-rho)` should then interleave every factor
root with the implicit carrier root. Exact inward exterior jets and additive
multiplicity parity should supply every physical sign cell.

Falsification includes a failed actual-source predecessor/product premise,
nonterminating sign refinement despite its verified nonzero premise, forged or
unbound field/sign/remainder acceptance, inconsistent strict carrier directions,
wrong rational coincidence, missing/duplicate roots, parity/exterior disagreement,
replacement proof search in the checker, or changed original source/authority.
A resource limit is not a truth result. A missing algorithm does not imply
mathematical impossibility.
