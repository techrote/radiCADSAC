## Bounded theorem and falsification reasoning

The exact algebra admitted by #397 is legitimate for a source whose original cosine coefficients satisfy
\[
F(t)=H_0(t)+H_1(t)\cos(2\pi\beta(t))
=P(u)^2\cos^2(\pi\beta(t))+Q(u)^2\sin^2(\pi\beta(t)),
\]
where \(\beta(t)\) is rational affine with nonzero rate and the exact extracted original two-span \(P,Q\) are rational affine. Its zero enumeration follows from simultaneous vanishing of the nonnegative squares. **This study does not refute that theorem.**

It instead proves that the theorem's necessary **input-shape contract** has empty intersection with the exact frozen 121-case corpus. Therefore direct reuse **cannot by itself** reduce even one of the 20 refused historical routes or 30 missing ordered premises. It does not follow that no *different* theorem, algebraic transformation, redesigned owner/consumer contract or future source could benefit from the square technique. In particular, no root/nonroot value for a rejected sine-bearing original has been inferred.

**No attempt to use sign sampling, timeouts, derivative-bound failures or a bounded resource limit as a mathematical counterexample.** The rejection is an exact finite structural membership decision.
