## Next theorem problem — proposal, **not an accepted solver**

For a single smooth original-source normalized span, let C_h(s), S_h(s) be rational polynomials, let the source phase be rational affine alpha+beta*s, and define

\[
F(s)=\sum_{h\ge0}\left[C_h(s)\cos(2\pi h(\alpha+\beta s))+S_h(s)\sin(2\pi h(\alpha+\beta s))\right],\qquad s\in[0,1].
\]

The **general analytic event theorem task** is to establish an *effectively terminating source-bound decision* for the identity-zero case versus all isolated open/endpoint physical roots, including their exact count, order, multiplicity/parity and one-sided sign cells; or to prove precisely why a proposed restricted method does not meet that contract. This formula describes a **target**, not evidence that the general bounded exponential/trigonometric zero problem is decidable by the project.

Candidate research approaches (unselected) may investigate rational analytic/Taylor interval enclosures with independent signed/root controls and symbolic polynomial/trigonometric structure. **Decisive falsifiers:** tangencies/even roots invisible to sign-change searches; arbitrarily close roots; exact identity equality; non-terminating interval refinement; source-knot continuity/one-sided order confusion; inability to prove a finite algorithm terminates for the admitted complete grammar. A numerical scan, arbitrary subdivision cap, or resource exhaustion is never exact zero/nonzero evidence.

The *separate secondary theorem interface* is ordered carrier views and physical sign transfer for V6–V18 plus nested V27/V41 child consumption. Counting roots, proving a carrier shares a zero set, or showing an auxiliary chart ratio monotone cannot establish global physical order by itself.
