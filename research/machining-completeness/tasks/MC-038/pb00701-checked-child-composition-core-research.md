# MC-038 / PB-007-01 — conditional exact cut/knot composition core (rational-polynomial subset)

**Date:** 29 October 2026. **Owner:** [MC-038 issue #100](https://github.com/techrote/radiCADSAC/issues/100). **Baseline:** `8171130cd47a4e8af2fd92f70f2c86213d9a110c` (reviewed/merged [PR #292](https://github.com/techrote/radiCADSAC/pull/292)). **Evidence type:** deterministic mathematical subset/model, **not** a historical V27/V41 child-consumption acceptance or production geometry result.

## Objective and decisive counterexample

Explore the smallest independently checkable physical composition *core* needed by the outstanding V27/V41 ordered-child interface: complete root order, endpoint/root multiplicity, one-sided signs at artificial certificate cuts, real original source knots, and exact canonical root-count deduplication. In the actual V27/V41 historical route, child classifiers largely provide **root counts**, not source-bound ordered physical witnesses. Therefore count-only reports must remain insufficient. This prototype **does not read those reports**, call their selecting classifier, or interpret unverified child claims as certification.

**Conditional hypothesis:** for a finite, continuous sequence of nonzero rational polynomials in one common global source parameter, if every polynomial comes with an **algebraically checked complete factorization into rational linear factors**, the exact global distinct roots, their multiplicities inside smooth spans, one-sided signs, and maximal sign cells can be composed across any finite rational proof cuts and true source knots without interpolation, epsilon comparison, guessed arithmetic type, or false global smoothness. **Falsifiers:** incomplete factorization, forged multiplicity or coefficient, discontinuity at a source knot, missing/overlapping source span, zero polynomial interval, unsupported irrational/nonlinear factor, resource exhaustion, an unaccounted physical event at a cut, or a source/authority mismatch.

## What is actually checked

Input schema `radicadsac-mc038-checked-rational-child-composition/1.0` accepts only `FULLY_SPLIT_RATIONAL_POLYNOMIAL_IN_GLOBAL_SOURCE_PARAMETER`. Every input rational is a canonical, bounded decimal-integer or reduced rational **string**, never a float. The source has an explicit `source_parameter_id`, ordered contiguous original source spans, and per-span polynomial coefficients (ascending order, in the **global** parameter). Each factor witness supplies a nonzero rational scale and a complete set of distinct rational roots with positive integer multiplicities. The checker multiplies all powers of `(s-r)` and requires **coefficient-by-coefficient equality** to the full source polynomial; it does not trust a declared root count or use a historical selecting route.

For each original source knot, the checker evaluates the two **independent exact polynomials** at the shared rational endpoint and requires equality. A knot at which the physical field is zero has *two separately reported one-sided orders*, and **no fabricated global smooth multiplicity**. A zero at a proof cut inside one polynomial instead inherits the *same* exact factor order from both sides. A nonzero proof cut generates **no** physical event. All proof cuts are sorted and deduplicated, including source-knot coincidences. A complete global physical root set is the sorted exact union of in-span factored roots. Signed open intervals are computed by rational midpoint evaluation, with completeness justified by the fully checked factorization and the absence of another factor root in the interval. Maximal physical sign cells ignore artificial nonzero proof cuts. Signed-jet parity at smooth roots independently checks crossing versus tangency.

The emitted `source_binding_sha256` is a deterministic digest of the **submitted** source-parameter ID, intervals and polynomial coefficients. It **is not proof** that these polynomials equal a historical modulated-trigonometric B-spline. Such upstream authenticity must be independently established in a future source-specific bridge. The output deliberately says `CONDITIONAL_EXACT_RATIONAL_SUBSET_COMPOSED`, *not* `CERTIFIED`; fields explicitly keep `v27_v41_trigonometric_children_certified=false`, `pb00701_certified=false`, `mc_b_established=false`, `mc1_established=false`, and `production_authorized=false`.

## Exact examples and adversarial controls

Primary two-span source on `s∈[0,2]`:

- `[0,1]`: `P0=(s−1/2)(s−1)`; a simple crossing at `s=1/2`, plus a first-order zero at the real source knot `s=1`.
- `[1,2]`: `P1=−(s−1)(s−3/2)^2`; the source stays continuous at `s=1`, but the right-hand first derivative differs. The knot zero is one physical event with separately verified left/right orders and signs. `s=3/2` is a multiplicity-2 tangency, including when it is also a proposed proof cut.

The complete physical roots are `1/2, 1, 3/2` (three distinct interior roots, not duplicated at the knot/cut); open physical sign cells are `+,−,−,−`. With the second piece sign inverted the same source knot is a sign-changing physical zero but remains **not** a globally smooth simple root claim. Further controls cover endpoint roots, a constant-sign source with arbitrary redundant proof cuts, exact factor order independence, forged multiplicities and source coefficients, inconsistent real knots, root-count/owner metadata injection, unknown trigonometric source kind, an unreduced rational or float, incomplete irreducible-polynomial factorization, identity-zero intervals, and typed non-truth resource refusal. ## Formal reasoning scope

On each smooth span, exact factor identity `P(s)=c∏(s−r_i)^{m_i}` (with `c≠0`) proves that the supplied `r_i` are *all* complex roots, hence every real physical root in the span, with their exact multiplicities. Between consecutive roots there are no zero values; continuity therefore fixes one sign, obtained at one exact rational midpoint. At a smooth zero `r`, the one-sided signs differ exactly when `m_i` is odd. At a real original source knot, polynomial equality at the knot proves continuity, but not differentiability: count a shared zero once, retain the independent left/right jets, and derive the physical one-sided sign behavior without assigning an unsupported global order. Rational proof cuts only refine intervals; they cannot add zeros. These steps establish composition for **this restricted class only**.

A polynomial such as `s²+1`, without a complete rational-linear factorization, is **not accepted** as a root-free result by this checker. Identity-zero source intervals return a typed `BLOCKED` non-truth outcome; degree/span/cut caps return `RESOURCE_REFUSAL` with `is_truth_value=false`. Invalid factor identities or structural metadata raise `InvalidWitness`. None is converted into a certificate.
