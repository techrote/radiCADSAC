## Exact generality proposition and original physical counterexample

The v4 source-solid representation `pb00703_source_codec.py` allows finite rational polynomial Boolean predicates with a `frame_id`, but *does not itself provide* a proof-preserving operation to move **any** such source through an exact reorientation of a machining setup. Relabelling its frame or replacing the resulting solid with a rotated axis-aligned box changes exact cutting truth. The original immutable MC-012 **F07** lathe–mill–lathe fixture exhibits the necessity: its `S-F07-M` setup rotates machine +Z to common +X while the original `F07-M1` cylindrical side-mill removes `(17,0,30)` but retains `(0,17,30)`, at equal radius. Non-cutting reclamping preserves durable body `B-F07`; frame reorientation must not silently rebuild it.

The new lowerer works on every finite v4 semialgebraic source **whose polynomial expansion remains within the explicitly reported resource budget**, regardless of nonconvex Boolean composition, role `stock/cutting/holder`, or source orientation. It admits all exact rational **proper orthogonal** 3×3 matrices and exact rational translations, not just signed axis permutations. This is an expansion of the **source-side semantic and constructor coverage**, not an admission of all possible physically realizable tool/stock descriptions.

The theorem is constructive. If `X = R u + t`, with `R^T R = I` and `det R = +1`, then for an original polynomial atom `P(u) <= 0` or `P(u) >= 0`, the transformed atom is **exactly** `P(R^T(X-t)) <= 0` or `>= 0`. Because rigid transformations are bijections, this equivalence commutes with finite union, intersection, ordered difference and complement. The latter is interpreted inside the finite source bounds, as in v4; the output world bounds are the image's conservative coordinate envelope of the *original bounds*, while the **pulled-back atom/Boolean AST is always the exact material predicate**. This envelope is not an AABB replacement for the cutter. Invertibility ensures nonzero source polynomial atoms stay nonzero. No tolerance or tessellation is introduced.

## Implementation and certificate

`pb00703_rigid_pose_20261109.py` builds an independent task-local exact polynomial pullback atop the unmodified historical v4 codec. Each output source binds the instance source ID, unchanged role/units/source uncertainty, named target frame and exact transformed finite bounds. The receipt binds original canonical v4 SHA-256, output canonical SHA-256, original and instance identities, rational pose/matrix/frame, independent pose SHA-256 and explicitly false access/native/STEP/PB-discharge flags. A verifier **replays the entire construction from the original source and pose**, rejecting output mutation, pose drift, source role/source identity drift and forged receipt claims.

Resource budgets are deliberately conservative: 128 KiB input, 1024 AST nodes, 4096 input and 8192 output polynomial terms, polynomial total degree at most 12, 512 bits per exact rational numerator/denominator and 1 MiB output. They bound this implementation, **not what physical machining permits**. Exceeding a budget returns `BLOCKED/PB-007-03/RESOURCE_BUDGET_OPEN`; this does not mean a valid object becomes invalid material. Invalid source/persistent-frame/pseudo-rotation/binary-float input returns `SEMANTIC_BLOCKER`, not exact authority. Such returns cannot discharge MC-B. No native/paid campaign is requested.

## Residual generality boundary and consumer handoff

This is **not a universal arbitrary-geometry source codec**. General irrational-angle continuous orientation, imported STEP/NURBS solids without exact source semantics, unconstrained arbitrary form and nonconvex geometry beyond a reviewed source grammar, geometric degeneracy requiring source-closedness/regularization, finite-progress of full simultaneous/tool-dependent sweeps, and verified complete holder/workholding/access clearance remain open. In particular this proof does not verify F07's actual multi-process material/body evolution or that its holder clears the stock. A source describes only *nominal* geometry and a retained journal ID; it cannot claim access from a Boolean intersection.

The follow-on consumer is the MC-020/MC-031 sweep/material route and ultimately #397's integration lane; it must consume posed **cutting and holder separately** and retain original operation identity, setup, distinct source uncertainty and body selection. All unsupported source classes must propagate `PB-007-03`, rather than disappear from the admitted domain. Actual native geometry, usable conventional STEP and machining completeness require separate evidence.
