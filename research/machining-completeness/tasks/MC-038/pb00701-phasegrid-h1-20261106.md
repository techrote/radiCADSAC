The admitted physical source is

Splines must have exact original full-clamped knot sequences and matching coefficient values at the true knot. No rephasing, normalized phasor norm, source projection or discarded sine channel is permitted.

Let the original knots, including exterior boundaries, be \(e_j\). Exact admission requires \(4(\alpha+\beta e_j)\in\mathbb Z\) for every \(e_j\). Define all intermediate rational phase nodes \(t_k=(k/4-\alpha)/\beta\) in original physical order, including the actual knots. The phase index \(k\) decreases when \(\beta<0\); signed-time ordering is still increasing.

At every such node,
\[

\begin{cases}

These are **exact rational physical signs**, not sampled floating-point trig approximations. A zero node is deliberately refused; the theorem does not silently classify it as a simple crossing.

## Certified local uniqueness, parity and signed-cell proof

On each rational phase-grid subcell, use a local parameter \(u\in[0,1]\). Set \(C=c_0+(c_1-c_0)u\), \(S=s_0+(s_1-s_0)u\), \(q=C^2+S^2\), and \(W=c_0(s_1-s_0)-s_0(c_1-c_0)\). Exact rational endpoint and interior-vertex evaluation yields \(q_{\min},q_{\max}\). Require \(q_{\min}>0\). Thus a continuous orientation \(\varphi=\operatorname{atan2}(S,C)\) exists on the subcell and

\[
F=\sqrt q\cos\delta,\quad
\delta=\theta-\varphi,\quad
\frac{d\delta}{du}=2\pi\,\beta\,(t_{k+1}-t_k)-\frac Wq.
\]

The strict **rational** bounds \(3<\pi<22/7\) give a closed enclosing interval for this derivative: for \(h=\beta(t_{k+1}-t_k)\), the phase term lies between \(6h\) and \(44h/7\), with endpoints ordered by value for negative \(h\). For \(W\ge0\), \(W/q\) lies between \(W/q_{\max}\) and \(W/q_{\min}\); for \(W<0\), between \(W/q_{\min}\) and \(W/q_{\max}\). Subtract interval bounds to obtain strict rational \((L,U)\).

**Sufficient admission test:** either \(L>0\) or \(U<0\), and \(\max(|L|,|U|)<3<\pi\). Thus \(\delta\) is strictly monotone and traverses less than \(\pi\) per subcell. Distinct cosine zeros are separated by \(\pi\); hence at most one physical zero lies inside. Any such zero is simple because \(q>0\) and \(\delta'\ne0\). Two nonzero exact endpoint signs determine its parity: opposite signs imply **exactly one** simple crossing, equal signs **zero**. No root search, tolerance, interval exhaustion or assumed exact transcendental root coordinate is used.

At a true original knot, coefficient continuity plus one global rational phase law verifies continuous physical \(F\). Because the admitted grid-node value is nonzero, neighboring signed cells join correctly. All distinct root intervals are rational, disjoint and physically ordered. The global maximal nonzero signed cells are the alternating signs constructed by actual certified crossings.

Refusals, which are **NONTRUTH**: shared H1 zero or identity, a physical zero at any phase-grid node or knot, unproved derivative direction, derivative drift budget not strictly below \(\pi\), unsupported H0/harmonics/higher degree/other time laws, discontinuity, nonlattice phase boundary, malformed or over-budget source.
