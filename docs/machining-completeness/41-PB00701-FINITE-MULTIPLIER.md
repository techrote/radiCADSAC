# PB-007-01 v15 — exact finite strict-dominance multiplier route

## Contract

This document records a bounded constructive extension of `PB-007-01` after v14. It applies to finite exact rational-polynomial lowered odd-harmonic source spans that prove, from their own coefficient maps, the normalized factorization

`F=(lambda_0+sum_{k=1}^m lambda_k*cos(2*k*alpha))*(A*cos(alpha)+B*sin(alpha))`,

with finite `m>=3`, `alpha=2*pi*h*theta_turn`, positive integer `h`, exact rational-polynomial carrier components `A,B`, and exact rational multiplier coefficients. `PB-007-01` remains **OPEN** outside this family.

The highest nonzero multiplier coefficient is normalized to one only by absorbing its exact scale into the carrier reconstructed from the highest source channels. No factored caller representation is authority. The source must prove an odd-harmonic lattice `{h,3h,...,(2m+1)h}`; wrong, even or non-lattice nonzero harmonics fail closed.

For `c_r=C_{(2r+1)h}/A` and `s_r=S_{(2r+1)h}/B`, exact product-to-sum identities give `lambda_0=(c_0+s_0)/2`, `lambda_1=c_0-s_0`, and for every interior index `lambda_r=c_r+s_r`, `lambda_{r+1}=c_r-s_r`. The highest ratios are exactly `1/2`. Every overlapping inference must agree and every source coefficient polynomial is regenerated and checked exactly. Missing intermediate channels may denote exact zero only; epsilon or near-zero classification is forbidden.

## Nonvanishing authority

The sole v15 global certificate is the exact rational strict-dominance condition

`|lambda_0|>sum_{k>=1}|lambda_k|`.

It yields a strictly positive exact margin and a constant multiplier sign because `|cos(2*k*alpha)|<=1`. Equality and sub-threshold cases remain blocked. Binary floating point, approximate `pi`, numerical trigonometry, sampling, minimization, epsilon, timeout and resource exhaustion are not truth authority.

A certified multiplier is smooth and globally nonzero, so the original multi-harmonic event has exactly the carrier zero set and finite multiplicities. The carrier is delegated only to established v10/v11/v12 authority; v8 retains ownership of a genuine nonconstant carrier common factor. v14 and all earlier routes retain precedence and are not rewritten.
