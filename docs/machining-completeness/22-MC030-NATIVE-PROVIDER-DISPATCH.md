# MC-030 — Native provider admission and total noncycling dispatch

MC-030 freezes the provider-routing contract used by later candidate selection. It does **not** establish MC-B, qualify a native kernel, or replace canonical material semantics with B-rep validity.

## Authority boundary

Canonical material remains the source-bound programme material relation tied to the immutable operation journal, exact input revision and durable `body_id`. A kernel-valid B-rep can still be materially wrong; kernel topology is not durable identity or lineage; STEP/B-rep write success is not independent engineering-output qualification. Fast-provider success therefore requires an independent material certificate.

## Fast paths

Four bounded fast paths are admitted only by sufficient predicates, never by operation labels:

- `FP-MILL-ANALYTIC-TRANSLATION` — MC-018/019 exact-rational fixed-axis stationary/line/polyline flat, corner-radius and ball/round sweeps with complete cutter region and no unresolved MC-058 shell.
- `FP-FORM-BOX-UNION` — MC-020 exact-rational box-union form/undercut sweeps; accessible undercuts require complete cutter-plus-holder access evidence.
- `FP-LATHE-AXISYMMETRIC` — MC-021 exact rotational reduction with certified target axisymmetry, coaxial setup, phase-independent meridian and full phase orbit.
- `FP-LATHE-PHASE-BOUNDED` — MC-022 finite synchronized/eccentric subtype with shared exact time, unwrapped phase and no unresolved transcendental event.

All material-changing operations retain MC-023 common-frame, exact-revision and durable-body binding. Re-clamps are non-cutting and do not reset inherited error.

## General route and fallback

Every source-bound request not admitted by exactly one fast path goes to `GENERAL_CERTIFIED_ROUTE`. A fast provider may be invoked once; on failure, uncertainty, or success without its independent material certificate it falls back exactly once to the general route. The general route cannot dispatch back to a fast path, so routing cannot cycle.

The general route is total as a **routing** relation, not as a success theorem. Its terminal outcomes include certified success, `PB-007-01`, `PB-007-02`, `PB-007-03`, `UNCERTIFIED`, `RESOURCE_REFUSAL` and `SEMANTIC_BLOCKER`. None of the latter states is success.

Directional, mesh/Manifold and sparse-volume/level-set candidates remain derived challengers or accelerators only. They cannot become canonical material authority through dispatch.

## Retained boundaries

Exact-zero/singular output, body-transition ownership, unresolved analytic events, arbitrary form/undercut source-codec gaps and unqualified engineering-output cases route out of the fast paths rather than being healed, sampled, simplified or dropped. `PB-007-01`, `PB-007-02`, `PB-007-03`, `RB-016-02` and `RB-016-04` remain open.

MC-A is `ACCEPTED`; MC-B through MC-F and MC-1 remain `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. No native or paid campaign was run. Source/audio/provenance, positive-volume material, canonical-journal authority and durable body/lineage semantics remain protected.
