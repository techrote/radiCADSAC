## Governing outcome

For every admissible finitely specified history in the independently defined conventional lathe and fixed-axis three-axis mill domain, provide a correct finite route to nominal material, topology and conventional usable engineering output at the admitted accuracy request. Include repeated/reoriented setups, simultaneous XYZ, accessible form/undercut tooling, ordinary phase-sensitive lathe work, degeneracies and all finite compositions. A physically valid awkward intersection is not an unsupported operation. Do not add arbitrary mathematical surfaces, continuous five-axis orientation, additive/deformation/force simulation or a full workshop to this tranche.

Three non-compensating results are required: constructive domain coverage; faithful native material-to-engineering realization; and practical qualification on independently preregistered workloads. Neither a proof without executable realization, an impressive finite score without coverage, nor a complete but unusable implementation satisfies all three.

## Boundaries and architecture

Keep a small headless research harness: canonical input and physical witness; independently interpreted cutter/setup/motion; actual sweep/material computation; checkable material/topology certificates; analytic or qualified patch reconstruction; conventional B-rep; STEP write/fresh read/independent consumer; programme-owned verdicts. Reuse suitable isolated worker infrastructure. No Godot/UI/game loop, production database/RPC/scheduler, marketplace, installer or production repository is authorized.

No universal winner is preselected. Source-screen exact/cell, interval/implicit, multi-directional, mesh, sparse-volume and native B-rep/process-specialized roles. Use small decisive failures before full prototypes. Advance one primary design and one truly independent challenger after screening. A useful incomplete accelerator may survive; it cannot be sole authority. Hybrid handoffs must preserve semantics, topology, body identity and error bounds, and their covered domains must actually cover the target.
