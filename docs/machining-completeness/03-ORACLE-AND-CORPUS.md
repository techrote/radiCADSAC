## Material comparison

Certificates must relate the candidate geometry B to nominal material M, not only compare scalar volumes. A checked common sandwich `L ⊆ M ⊆ U` and `L ⊆ B ⊆ U` bounds their symmetric difference inside `U \\ L`; a certified volume of that band can bound missing/extra material. It does not establish distance or topology. Use stronger directional enclosures or exact comparison where needed. A broad interval containing both correct and materially wrong answers is inconclusive.

Record outward rounding, precision, evaluator bounds, local hypotheses and aggregation. Omitted unresolved cells, unproved numeric error and candidate-supplied `SOLVED` strings cannot pass. Prefer a small independently reviewed checker when its certificate statement actually covers the claim.

## Coverage and metamorphic relations

Vary signed gaps/penetrations with exact zero separate from positive/negative neighbours; tool radius; feature-to-stock scale; orientation and phase; segment/unique-work counts; retrace/noise; event density; and reconstruction precision. Do not quantize a stress case into an easier case to fit an old profile.

Require a witness for every admitted constructor and closure/interface lemma, pairwise mechanism coverage and targeted higher-order chronological compositions. Generated finite samples challenge the universal argument; they do not define D.

Check exact-retrace idempotence, subdivision equivalence, qualified rigid-transform covariance, mm/in equivalence and context-qualified order/union relations. Reversal is valid only when the effective cutting set and engagement are unchanged. Time reparameterization is not generally invariant for phase-sensitive turning. Re-clamping equivalence preserves body targets and setup meaning. Signed perturbations are distinct expected outcomes, not approximate equalities.

## Negative controls and failure retention

Reject lost thin bodies, added bridges, filled channels, wrong units, wrong sweep endpoints, false no-ops, reset error budgets, topology/identity mismatch, omitted unresolved cells, changed source hash, invalid bounds, stale caches and mesh-only STEP substitution. Include defects small enough to pass a coarse volume tolerance.

Freeze original input, competing outputs and exact configurations on disagreement. Minimize without deleting the machining fact responsible. Diagnose physical semantics before numerical/implementation differences. No library or consumer is default truth. An oracle repair versions the oracle and invalidates/reruns affected claims; it does not rewrite expectations to match a favourite candidate.

Every failure/timeout/unexecuted cell remains in an append-only ledger. No mandatory failure leaves the denominator without a reviewed physical/domain correction.
