## Purpose and exact scope

V49 can consume a finite mixed rational/algebraic partition only when each
closed child has a complete strict derivative certificate. The same smooth
source cannot have incompatible strict derivative signs at a shared cut.
A double physical root is therefore not solved by finer orientation partitioning.

V50 instead proves the physical identity F=gH and composes its root sets.
The maximal monic rational amplitude GCD g may vanish, while the modulated
carrier H must possess a complete independently checked predecessor certificate
that makes every carrier root simple. F itself need not be monotone: an even
factor root is a physical tangency, never a strict-derivative success for F.

The finite carrier-checker scope is explicit: v19 monotone harmonic-zero anchor,
v47 algebraic single-cut, v48 corrected mixed-root single-cut, and v49 mixed
multi-cut. Complete v49 selects the carrier's owner first. Other certified
owners are not silently relabelled; they receive a precise checker-scope blocker.
Complete predecessor successes on F itself retain their identical original
results. No new v50 recursion is used to qualify H.

This is mathematical root-event model evidence, not native geometry, a universal
nonmonotone solver, a new machining-domain restriction, or production permission.

## Source identity and maximal factor

On one already-lowered parent coordinate t, retain the physical source

F(t)=C0(t)+sum_h(C_h(t) cos(2*pi*h*(o+r*t))+S_h(t) sin(2*pi*h*(o+r*t))).

All amplitudes are rational polynomials, positive harmonics are integers in a
finite set, and o,r are rational with r nonzero. Regenerate this material from
historical source validation/lowering. Harmonic-zero sine is identically zero.
Take the monic Euclidean GCD of EVERY nonzero physical amplitude, including C0
and all non-anchor channels. An all-zero identity is handled separately. A
constant GCD is not new v50 authority.

For each original amplitude p, exact division gives p=g*q with zero remainder;
exact multiplication reconstructs p coefficientwise. The original p remains
in the physical record. Monic normalization transfers any scale/sign to q,
not out of the physical source. The quotient amplitudes have GCD one. The
source id, global source interval and global rational-affine phase law are
unchanged. Exact clamped-Bernstein controls supply the historical admitted
B-spline source representation of H; they are not a new source grammar.

## Physical multiplicity and distinct-set union

Analytic functions with finite vanishing orders m and n factor locally as
(t-x)^m u(t) and (t-x)^n v(t), where u(x)v(x) is nonzero. Consequently
ord_x(F)=ord_x(g)+ord_x(H). A nonzero factor contributes order zero.
This also proves crossing for odd total order and tangency for even total order.

## Irrational factor/carrier disjointness

Let mu be alpha's irreducible rational minimal polynomial. If every quotient
amplitude vanished at alpha, mu would divide every quotient amplitude. This
contradicts their maximal-GCD-derived coprimality. The implementation additionally
regenerates every quotient value and Laurent coefficient in the selected real
field, so this premise is executable, not a caller's claim.

The local phase tau=o+r*alpha is algebraic irrational. Apply the same explicitly
scoped theorem as v46: Gelfond-Schneider at base -1, exponent 2*tau, logarithm
i*pi makes z=exp(2*pi*i*tau) transcendental. A nonzero algebraic-coefficient
Laurent polynomial in z cannot vanish. For the complete finite commensurate
harmonic source, collecting the cosine/sine coefficients in Q(alpha,i) yields
such a nonzero Laurent polynomial. Thus H(alpha) is nonzero, g's irrational
roots are disjoint from H's roots, and physical ord(F) equals ord(g) there.

Reference: Ivan Niven, *Irrational Numbers*, Chapter X, Theorem 10.1, DOI
`10.5948/9781614440116.011`, and the accepted v46 endpoint proof. The hypotheses
are essential: nonzero rational-affine phase, integer commensurate harmonics,
algebraic amplitude values and a nonzero collected Laurent polynomial.
No conclusion is made for independent phase laws or a rational exponent.

V46's exact amplitude-jet implementation independently regenerates the physical
F multiplicity and the nonzero H endpoint jet. This new endpoint interface is
owned by the source factor g. It never invents A/B orientation ownership for H,
never feeds a mismatched bisection to the historical v46 checker, and does not
search for H's trigonometric sign at alpha. V8's older constant-quotient scope
and v18's nonvanishing-factor scope remain unchanged historical evidence.

## Counts, signs and correspondence

For the initial strict carrier family there is at most one open H root. The
physical distinct open count is Ng+Nh-Ncoincidence; irrational coincidences are
excluded above and rational ones are exactly decided. Every factor event has
its exact physical multiplicity and parity. Any carrier-only root remains
simple because g is nonzero there. Multiple-root counts and crossing/tangency
counts follow from this checked union, not from the carrier's count alone.

Physical exterior signs are regenerated directly from F and checked against
sign(g)*sign(H). Exterior multiplicities include both factors. A non-unit global
source interval of positive rational width scales jets by width^(-m) and keeps
multiplicity unchanged; the implementation records that exact scale.

The carrier-only roots retain their predecessor witness/cell correspondence.
This package does NOT claim a single globally ordered explicit analytic-root
list interleaving factor and carrier roots; such ordering may require additional
carrier sign evidence. It establishes the stated exact set union, counts,
multiplicities and endpoint semantics, not an unimplemented ordered-root API.

## Source-regenerating checker and refusal

The checker regenerates maximal factorization from original physical source,
then validates the supplied carrier witness using its finite owner-specific
checker. It never calls the carrier classifier to find a replacement proof.
Every factor-root record, algebraic field, Laurent/jet/disjointness premise,
rational coincidence, exterior and final union is regenerated and compared by
type-sensitive canonical JSON. Root isolation and finite arithmetic may be
recomputed; derivative-route selection and transcendental sign search are not
used to rescue forged evidence.

The public adapter runs complete v49 first and upgrades only exact lowered
residual spans. Unknown source fields, prior successes and unrelated exceptions
retain predecessor behavior. Resources at any new stage return typed non-truth.
There is no approximate factor/cut, epsilon, floating-point trig, denominator
cap, refinement depth or timeout-as-correctness rule, and no global monkeypatch.
Finite polynomial degrees bound GCD/division and multiplicity jets; existing
exact root separation/minimal-field authority provides root/sign termination.
