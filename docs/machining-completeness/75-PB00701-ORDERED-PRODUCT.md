## Scope and authority

For one already-lowered rational polynomial source span, v50 qualifies a physical
function `F(t)=g(t)H(t)` by exact source factorization, polynomial factor roots,
and a checked strict/simple carrier. Its root-set union counts, multiplicities,
rational coincidences and irrational disjointness do not themselves order every
physical root. V50 explicitly keeps `ordered_full_analytic_root_list_claimed=false`.
This version adds a separate ordered-evidence interface; it does not rewrite that
historical result or pretend the physical product is strictly monotone.

The source is the complete harmonic-zero/cosine/sine polynomial map, source ID,
original global source interval and rational-affine phase law. The polynomials
are expressed in the original normalized parent coordinate `t in [0,1]`.
The global source coordinate is `u=lo+(hi-lo)t`, with `hi>lo`. Source polynomial
normalization and physical scaling remain those already proved by v50.

The bounded carrier scope is unchanged: a full closed-span derivative witness,
with one consistent strict sign, from the actual v19, v47, v48 or v49 owner. An
unchecked, nonmonotone or unsupported carrier is not admitted because its root
count looks convenient. This is a solver coverage boundary, not permission to
remove operations from the physical domain or relabel valid input as invalid.

## Factor-owned carrier sign

At an irrational root `alpha` of `g`, first regenerate the original v50 factor
endpoint evidence. The source-derived maximal monic GCD leaves the rational
amplitudes of `H` coprime. They cannot all vanish at `alpha`: its rational minimal
polynomial would then divide every quotient amplitude. A nonzero rational-affine
phase at an algebraic irrational source gives an algebraic irrational exponent
in the scoped v46 Laurent/Gelfond-Schneider argument. The collected Laurent
polynomial over `Q(alpha,i)` is nonzero. Therefore `H(alpha) != 0`.

Only after that exact nonzero branch is proved does `_decide_factor_sign` refine
rational enclosures. It reuses v46's selected-field root refinement, exact Machin
pi enclosure, and sine/cosine Taylor polynomials with Lagrange remainder and
Lipschitz radius bounds. Every physical quotient amplitude contributes. Finite
precision `n` is doubled until the complete interval excludes zero strictly.
No sampled or floating-point sign is used, and no fixed refinement budget is a
truth rule. The point, finite harmonics, amplitude values and phase are fixed;
all enclosures converge. Together with proved nonvanishing this supplies finite
termination. Physical resources can still fail and remain non-truth.

A factor root need not be an orientation root of `H`. No quotient A/B owner or
historical orientation bisection is invented. The new sign record embeds the
factor-owned v50 proof and binds original `F`, `g`, `H`, field embedding, source
ID, phase and finite enclosure. It is explicitly endpoint-only, not an unchecked
whole-carrier certificate. Rational factor roots instead retain v50's exact
rational-turn evaluation, including its separate exact zero/coincidence branch.

## Ordering the implicit carrier root

Let `delta` be the sign of the checked derivative of `H` on the complete span.
V19 supplies its fixed derivative-dominance record; v47/v48 supply two closed
children; v49 supplies its complete mixed-boundary partition. Every child sign
and summary is regenerated by its own finite checker and must agree. Smoothness
belongs to this one lowered polynomial span, not to arbitrary spline knots.

If `rho` is the carrier's unique open root, strict monotonicity gives

```text
sign(delta * H(x)) = sign(x - rho).
```

For `x<rho`, integrate the strictly signed derivative, or apply the mean value
theorem, to obtain `delta*H(x)<0`; the reverse inequality holds for `x>rho`.
Equality is exactly the carrier root. Thus exact carrier signs at the already
sorted factor roots determine their order relative to `rho`, without evaluating
`rho` numerically or subtracting elements of different selected fields.

A rational zero is the existing coincident factor root. It appears once with
additive physical multiplicity. Irrational factor/carrier coincidence is excluded
by the preceding nonzero proof. Otherwise the signs identify one unique gap
between adjacent factor roots or exteriors containing `rho`. Its endpoints are
strictly ordered exactly, have opposite `H` signs, and enclose no factor root.
The checked carrier witness proves existence, uniqueness and simplicity there.

The carrier-only position is a `UNIQUE_ANALYTIC_ROOT` descriptor containing that
exact boundary pair, source/carrier/witness bindings and strict derivative sign.
Its arithmetic nature is **unclaimed**. It has no invented algebraic minimal
polynomial or decimal coordinate; it may in fact be rational. Its global image
is a symbolic exact positive affine image of the same root. This is sufficient
for an ordered event decomposition, but is not a general arithmetic interface
for implicit analytic numbers.

Finite-finite boundary ordering reuses v49's exact selected-field/isolation
comparison. Finite-implicit ordering uses the checked sign theorem. A cell may
have boundaries from different fields. It stores an ordered boundary pair, not
a fabricated common compositum or normalized mixed-field width. Overlapping
old isolations or old root-bearing proof children are not relative-order proofs.

## Physical sign cells and exterior jets

V50 already proves `ord_x(F)=ord_x(g)+ord_x(H)` and complete physical root counts.
The new ordered list retains those exact multiplicities. Between successive
physical roots a continuous nonzero real analytic function has constant sign.
Crossing a root of multiplicity `m` changes that sign by `(-1)^m`: odd roots
cross, even roots are tangencies.

An exterior zero alone cannot start this propagation. At the left exterior the
factor's first nonzero rational derivative gives the right-inward sign. At the
right exterior the same derivative sign is multiplied by `(-1)^m` for a leftward
approach. V49's exact `side_jet` supplies these original-source jets and full
multiplicities. The factorial is positive and therefore irrelevant to sign; the
global derivative scale `(hi-lo)^(-m)` is recorded and is also positive.

For a nonzero carrier exterior, its exact rational-turn sign is the inward sign.
For a carrier exterior root, checked simplicity and strict derivative give
`delta` on the left and `-delta` on the right. Multiply the factor and carrier
inward signs, propagate across each ordered open physical root by parity, and
require agreement with the independently regenerated right-inward sign.

`ordered_boundaries` includes both source exteriors, whether zero or nonzero.
`ordered_physical_events` includes only physical zeros, with endpoint roots once.
`sign_cells` are all intervening nonempty open intervals, with exact order/width
proofs, original global endpoints and sign. No proof cut becomes an extra root.
The assembled open/closed counts, multiple roots, crossing/tangency counts,
endpoint multiplicities and simplicity flag must exactly match the v50 product.

## Executable interfaces and finite checking

The versioned implementation lives under `research/machining-completeness/tasks/MC-038/`:

- `build_factor_carrier_sign(certificate, *original_source_args)` proves only the
  carrier sign at that source-factor-owned irrational point.
- `build_ordered_product_event(product, *original_source_args)` first validates
  the supplied whole v50 product, then creates the ordered evidence.
- `validate_ordered_product_event(candidate, *original_source_args)` checks the
  supplied product, finite signs and complete ordered composition without
  carrier selection, derivative selection or endpoint sign search.
- `build_ordered_source_evidence(spec)` runs the complete v50 source classifier
  first. Older successes and source rejections retain their original result.
  V50-owned certified spans get additional evidence, separately from the
  unchanged complete `physical_event_result`.

The finite truth checker accepts the **original lowered rational channels and
source/interval/phase**, like v50. It does not independently certify a fabricated
historical source-classifier routing envelope. The source wrapper obtains those
channels by actual predecessor lowering; every selected span's ordered proof is
then checked against them. Consumers must not treat a source-wrapper digest alone
as proof of source lowering. The distinction is explicit rather than replacing
historical classification with a guessed common envelope.

For irrational signs, the finite checker first regenerates nonzero/source/field
premises, independently audits Machin sums and analytic remainder formulas via
v46's direct-summation checker, and regenerates exactly **one** enclosure at the
supplied finite witness size. Failure cannot be rescued by another sign search.
Rational equality and the full factor/carrier evidence retain their existing
checkers. Exact typed JSON equality rejects omitted, duplicated, reordered or
unknown metadata, including Boolean-as-number and false capability fields.
The ordered composition is a shared deterministic formula, not a claimed wholly
independent geometry oracle; independent scalar series auditing and adversarial
source/ordering/parity expectations provide additional checks.

The source wrapper can return evidence for selected v50 spans while others keep
older owners. Its `all_source_spans_have_ordered_evidence` flag states that scope.
No cross-knot continuity, cross-span endpoint gluing or global cross-span union
is claimed. A new resource failure preserves the successful physical count
result separately; the ordered evidence is not successful. Unrelated exceptions
propagate rather than becoming false arithmetic/refusal facts.
