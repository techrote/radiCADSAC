## Exact certificate cuts

- COS boundaries are rational turns congruent to `1/6`, `1/3`, `2/3`, or `5/6` modulo one turn;
- SIN boundaries are rational turns congruent to `1/12`, `5/12`, `7/12`, or `11/12` modulo one turn.

For every source-derived mixed candidate harmonic `h`, v27 solves the affine equation

`h*(a+r*s)=boundary`

exactly over the rationals. Only solutions with `0 < s < 1` become internal certificate cuts. External endpoint equality is retained as an endpoint and never creates a zero-width child. Coincident cuts generated by different harmonics or boundary families are deduplicated by exact rational equality.

These are proof-partition boundaries, not approximate event boundaries. Caller cuts, sampling, numerical trigonometry, epsilon-expanded sectors, arbitrary subdivision depth, timeouts and resource limits have no correctness authority.

## Exact child reparameterization

For each adjacent exact cut pair `[a,b]`, every source polynomial is transformed under

`s=a+(b-a)u`, `u in [0,1]`

by exact rational binomial substitution. The resulting power polynomial is encoded as an exact clamped rational B-spline. The affine phase becomes

`phi_child(u)=phi(a)+r*(b-a)u`.

The shared `source_parameter_id` is preserved and the certificate records the exact mapping from every child back to the parent/global source interval.

No floating-point geometry, source reconstruction, resampling or tolerance widening occurs.

## Exact event composition

Adjacent children describe the same analytic source at an internal rational certificate cut, so their exact endpoint relations must agree. `NEGATIVE` and `POSITIVE` cuts are not roots. A shared `ZERO` cut is one global root, not two child roots.

An internal `ZERO` is counted exactly once as a parent-open root only when both neighboring child summaries independently prove endpoint multiplicity one. Child open-root counts are then summed, internal-cut roots are added once, and the two original external endpoint roots retain their established multiplicity semantics.

Certificate cuts do not alter the function, root set, source parameter, audio/provenance identity or operation history.
