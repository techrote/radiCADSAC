# MC-026 candidate selection and implementation blueprint

MC-026 selects a bounded material-evaluation architecture from the reviewed candidate evidence. It does not claim constructive completeness, native implementation acceptance, practical qualification or engineering-output qualification.

## Selected architecture

The general material route is `PRIMARY_CERTIFIED_ADAPTIVE_IMPLICIT`: certified adaptive interval/implicit material evaluation over the actual MC-018..023 source sweeps. Adaptive refinement is only a representation/refinement mechanism. Exact equality, multiplicity, singular/topology-critical events and phase-sensitive decisions require separately certified event evidence; cell width, refinement depth, global epsilon, binary floating point, timeout and resource caps cannot become correctness authority.

The independent challenger is `EXACT_CELL_SEMIALGEBRAIC_CONTROL`, limited to source instances with a proved finite semialgebraic encoding. It uses a separate material representation, classifier and certificate/result namespace and may not share adaptive-cell classification code. Challenger agreement strengthens evidence but is not a completeness proof and cannot close a blocker.

`DIRECTIONAL_INTERVAL_INDEX`, `NATIVE_MESH_BOOLEAN` and `SPARSE_LEVELSET` remain non-authoritative derived accelerators/challengers. Their samples, components, topology IDs, manifoldness or extraction success do not become canonical material, durable body/lineage or STEP authority.

## Dispatch

The MC-030 provider design remains the front end. The four bounded fast paths are retained under their exact sufficient predicates, fast `SUCCESS` requires an independent material certificate, and any failure or uncertified success falls exactly once to `PRIMARY_CERTIFIED_ADAPTIVE_IMPLICIT`. At most one fast provider and one general provider may run; the general provider never dispatches back to a fast provider.

Every one of the 26 required MC-002 operation IDs is partitioned exactly once into a lathe-axisymmetric, lathe-phase, mill-analytic, mill-form or composition/general routing class. Operation labels alone never establish fast-path admission. Form-source cases retain `PB-007-03`; phase-sensitive cases retain `PB-007-01` and `PB-007-02`; material-route selection does not close output blockers.

## Downstream leaf blueprints

MC-031 owns source-bound evaluator ingress/dispatch (`MC031-A`), the certified adaptive general evaluator (`MC031-B`), and non-authoritative accelerator adapters (`MC031-C`). `CTRL-026-A` supplies the exact-cell differential control inside the MC-031 evidence path.

MC-032 owns the exact algebraic event kernel (`MC032-A`), certified transcendental/phase event service (`MC032-B`), and finite-progress/blocker terminalization (`MC032-C`). `CERT-026-A` binds the frozen challenge/certificate identities without changing MC-049 custody semantics.

MC-033 owns the regularized durable-body state machine (`MC033-A`) and programme-owned lineage transition certificates (`MC033-B`). Backend topology handles, component order and proximity remain non-authoritative.

These are implementation blueprints under existing issues, not new task identities. MC-031/032/033 remain gated by MC-038 and MC-026 does not authorize native or paid execution.

## Challenge freeze and open obligations

The MC-049 challenge set remains 64 frozen candidate-blind records classified `PUBLIC_PRESELECTION_PREREGISTRATION`; it is not secret, independent or held-out and remains unexecuted here. MC-057 owns any later independent custody/execution.

`PB-007-01`, `PB-007-02`, propagated `PB-007-03`, `RB-016-02`, `RB-016-03` and `RB-016-04` remain open. `UNCERTIFIED`, `RESOURCE_REFUSAL` and `SEMANTIC_BLOCKER` remain valid fail-closed outcomes and cannot be rewritten as success.

MC-A remains accepted. MC-B, MC-C, MC-D, MC-E, MC-F and MC-1 remain `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

Historical source/audio/provenance remains immutable. Canonical journal intent remains immutable; positive-volume material may not be dropped by tolerance/refinement policy; durable body identity and lineage remain programme-owned rather than backend topology; conventional STEP remains mandatory downstream engineering output.
