# Genesis v1 → Genesis v2 handoff delta

RCS-027 creates new v2 packages; it does not edit or reinterpret the frozen Genesis-v1 package trees.

## Unchanged programme foundations

- `msac-journal/1.0` remains the durable manufacturing-intent schema.
- immutable revisions, durable material-body IDs/transitions and semantic lineage remain programme-owned;
- source/audio identity and provenance remain protected durable metadata;
- lathe and mill remain the founding process scope;
- STEP remains mandatory primary engineering output and defaults to all committed material bodies;
- kernel/provider/private topology types remain outside the stable MSAC↔OpenSimachinist boundary;
- pathological geometry, disconnected material and uncertainty are normal engineering cases, not permission to simplify truth.

## New explicit non-success states

Genesis v2 makes `accepted_pending`, ambiguous-lineage pending/refusal, `error_budget_breach`, `refused_accuracy_unproven`, provider capability refusal and `interoperability_unqualified` first-class outcomes. These are not regressions from “success”; they prevent plausible-looking derived geometry from silently becoming engineering authority.

## Deliberately not promoted

Genesis v2 does not claim universal five-axis milling, arbitrary tool-axis fallback, live-tool/eccentric turning, broad form-tool/holder qualification, exact analytic recovery of all tool-generated surfaces, fully qualified Layer-D STEP interoperability, topology-stable face IDs or production capacity SLAs.

Each is either routed to a qualified future provider, retained as bounded pending work, explicitly refused, or scheduled for later requalification.
